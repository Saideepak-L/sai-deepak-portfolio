Project 01

Local ordering website

Smash Guys

A direct collection-ordering experience with a custom burger builder and kitchen ticket workflow for a Hatfield food business.

My role
UX, interface design, front-end build, ordering flow
Technology
HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, Supabase, PrintNode
Core task
Make mobile ordering quick while keeping menu and kitchen operations manageable.
